# Privacy Policy - Table Tennis Ratings App

**Last updated:** July 30, 2026

## Overview

Table Tennis Ratings is an independent mobile app for looking up USA Table Tennis player ratings. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by USA Table Tennis. This policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data We Collect

- **Login Credentials:** Your email and password are sent to the USA Table Tennis servers (usatt.justgo.com) to authenticate you. We do not store your password on our servers.
- **Usage Data:** We log successful logins (timestamp and name) to track app usage. No other personal data is collected.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- Location data
- Contacts or photos
- Device identifiers for advertising
- Analytics or tracking beyond login counts

## Third-Party Services

The app connects to USA Table Tennis's official API (usatt.justgo.com) to retrieve player ratings. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your data.

## Data Storage

Your login credentials are stored securely on your device using the iOS Keychain (or Android EncryptedSharedPreferences). They are never transmitted to any server other than USA Table Tennis's official authentication endpoint.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: https://github.com/swapgd/tt/issues

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted to this page.
